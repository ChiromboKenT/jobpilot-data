About the opportunity


A UK-based creator-marketing business is looking for a Lead Software Engineer to take technical ownership of its campaign-management platform. 
The platform is already used by the internal team. You’ll help develop it into the operational product that campaign teams, creators and clients rely on, covering shortlisting, outreach, reporting, payments and related workflows. 
You’ll work alongside the Product Lead, an existing Full Stack Engineer and a Data Science Lead. As the senior engineering owner, you’ll set technical direction while continuing to code and deliver features yourself. 
This is a long-term opportunity to build the product, establish sound engineering practices and help grow the team. 

What you’ll own


 
 
Product delivery: Build and release complete features, incorporating feedback from real users.

 
 
 
Architecture: Make practical decisions about system design, scaling and maintainability.

 
 
 
AI engineering: Ship reliable LLM features and establish validation, evaluation and cost controls.

 
 
 
Production operations: Own monitoring, alerting, troubleshooting and reliability improvements.

 
 
 
Integrations: Connect CRM, payment, outreach, invoicing and third-party data services.

 
 
 
Engineering standards: Establish testing, code review, documentation, CI/CD and infrastructure-as-code practices.

 
 
 
Product partnership: Break down requirements, challenge priorities and communicate realistic delivery expectations.

 
 
 
Team development: Identify resourcing needs and help hire, mentor and lead future engineers.

Multimodal AI, API ingestion, email deliverability, SOC 2 readiness and creator-marketing experience are additional advantages.
